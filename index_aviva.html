The Developer Struggle

The Evolution of AI Strategy

Phase 1: Prompt Engineering

Focuses on "Clever Phrasing". It relies on the developer's ability to ask the right way every single time. (Fragile & Unscalable)

Phase 2: Context Engineering

Focuses on "Right Information". Bringing the data, standards, and rules to the AI in the right format automatically. (Durable & Trusted)

"Context engineering is focused less on clever phrasing and more on bringing the right information (in the right format) to the LLM."
