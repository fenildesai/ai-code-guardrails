# Pitch Deck Speaker Script: CodeReview.AI

This script is designed for a fast-paced, high-impact presentation (approx. 5-7 minutes).

---

## Slide 1: Title - CodeReview.AI
**The Hook:** 
"Do you know… waiting for pull‑request reviews is one of the top frustrations for software engineers worldwide? We spend thousands of hours building great code, only to let it sit idle. This is CodeReview.AI — the solution that reclaims that time."

---

## Slide 2: The Problem
**The Pain Point:**
"The numbers are staggering. Pull Requests sit idle for **50% of their lifecycle**. That's not just a statistic; it's 44% of our teams telling us this is their #1 bottleneck. Every hour an engineer spends waiting for a review is an hour they aren't innovating. Raising a PR take 1 minute... getting it reviewed shouldn't take days."

---

## Slide 3: The Solution
**The Vision:**
"We’ve built a solution that integrates directly into our existing Azure DevOps ecosystem. It provides **instant feedback** in minutes, not days. Most importantly? It’s **100% internal and secure**. No code leaves our network. We get inline, actionable comments exactly where we need them: in the PR."

---

## Slide 4: How It Works
**The Process:** 
*(Point to the diagram)*
"The flow is seamless. 
1. Developer creates a PR. 
2. Our build validation pipeline triggers automatically.
3. The pipeline calls our secure internal API. 
4. The model returns structured JSON feedback. 
5. Within 60 seconds, comments are posted directly back to the developer. 
It’s continuous, it's automated, and it never sleeps."

---

## Slide 5: The Path to Scale
**The Strategy:**
"We’ve already **proven** this with a successful POC in the Platform Team. 
"We’ve already **proven** this with a successful POC in the Bamboo team. 
Our next critical step is **Production**. We are currently focusing on the **Oasis (Endpoint Owners)** to ensure our security and governance are airtight.
Once we have that strategic alignment, we’re ready for a full enterprise-wide rollout."

---

## Slide 6: ROI Projection
**The Story:**
"As some of you know, we've been running a pilot with the **Bamboo team**. The results have been eye-opening. We didn't just see a slight improvement; we saw a fundamental shift in how they work. We’re reclaiming over **260 hours every single month**. That is **32.5 man-days** of engineering time put back into building features instead of waiting for reviews. 
That's **£195,000 in annual savings** for just one team. Scale that across the organization, and we’re looking at nearly half a million pounds in recovered productivity."

---

## Slide 7: Why We’ll Succeed
**The Reassurance:**
"Why this? Why now? 
First, **Security**. Unlike cloud alternatives, we are internal-first. 
Second, **Proven Tech**. We aren't reinventing the wheel; we're using native ADO APIs. 
Third, **Measurable Impact**. We can track velocity and quality metrics in real-time. We have the data to prove it works."

---

## Slide 8: Final Impact - Stop Waiting. Start Building.
**The Close:**
"We have a simple choice. We can keep losing 50% of our development time to bottlenecks, or we can empower our engineers to build faster. With CodeReview.AI, a PR is raised in one minute and reviewed in one minute. 
Let's stop waiting, and start building. Thank you."

### Pro-Tips:
- **Slide 5 (Production):** If asked about Oasis, emphasize that you are working with the *Endpoint Owners* on governance and security to ensure standard compliance.
- **Slide 6 (ROI Calculation):** If asked for the math behind the **£195,000**, here is the logic:
    - **260 Hours saved per month** (60 mins * 65 PRs/week * 4 weeks).
    - **32.5 Man-days reclaimed per month** (260 hours / 8h day).
    - **£16,250 Monthly savings** (32.5 days * £500 avg daily cost).
    - **£195,000 Annual savings** (£16,250 x 12 months).
- **Bamboo team Scale:** This represents just one focused team; organization-wide impact scales significantly.
